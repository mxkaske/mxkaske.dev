title: Making the best of it
date: 2026-11-23
description: I miss crafting UI components. But bootstrapping openstatus with Thibault gives me something more valuable.
---

I miss building UI components. I still do it, but in less obvious ways.

If I weren't all in on [openstatus](https://openstatus.dev), I'd be doing it all the time. I can lose hours polishing an interface. Hovering over the same button again and again until it feels just right.

Could I let AI handle that part while I push openstatus forward? Sure. But it wouldn't feel like _mine_.

It's okay that things change. Let them.

So instead, I'm learning about distributed systems. I'm digging into SEO. I'm listening to users. I'm keeping a production app alive at scale.

But the best part isn't technical. **It's my friendship with Thibault.** That's what bootstrapping openstatus really gives me: figuring out how to grow a business and going through the ups and downs together.

At the end of the day, if you're making the best of your situation, consider yourself a winner.
