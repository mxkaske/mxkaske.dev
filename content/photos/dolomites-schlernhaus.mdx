We started at Seis am Schlern and didn't plan to hike all the way up to the Schlernhaus, as it was already a bit of a longer hike. But at the junction, the way up was right in front of us, so we took it. Totally worth it. It's really steep, but rewarding once you're up there at 2.5k meters.
