---
title: Stop Taking Shortcuts
date: 2026-08-16
description: There is no shortcut to success.
---

It’s so funny, people tell you “SEO is dead”, “if you’re not letting your agents run your business 24/7 you’re not gonna make it”, yada yada yada.

Everyone tries to sell you their tool to be more productive, work faster, don’t miss out on things… bullshit. I often fell into the trap, but it’s all illusion.

Make your repeated work better yourself.

There is no shortcut to success. Work and focus consistently on what you want to grow.

Stop taking shortcuts
