---
title: Where You're Coming From
date: 2026-09-11
description: The numbers going up is the easy part.
---

The numbers go up. The feedback is good. Motivation is high. That's the easy part.

Remember where you're coming from. Remember who was there for the hardest moments - most of them didn't happen at work. Remember who you met on the way. Some of your decisions turned out to be really good ones. That's not a reason to stand taller. It's a reason to stay humble and give back.

Giving back is how the connection grows. It won't be easy - the daily business doesn't pause for it. Don't expect anything in return. Be yourself, be critical, share feedback. That's how a team works. That's how a community works. That's how society works.

_Zeus law_: treat everyone with the same respect. No exceptions for titles, numbers, or timing.

And when the numbers go up again, ask the only question that matters: what does success even mean?
